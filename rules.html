Winter Light

Rules and rubric

The call is open now. Entries close December 15, 2026, at 11:59 PM CT. Results post by December 22, 2026.

How to enter

Entry is $5 and covers up to three works. There are no other fees. Pay the fee through the Stripe checkout. After you pay, you’ll be sent to a page with the email address and the list of what to include with your images.

Enter for $5

Organizer, sales, and contact

Winter Light is organized by Beauty Exhibit (beautyexhibit.art). Bots do a first pass on each entry against the rubric below, and the show’s director makes the final picks. There is no named juror or jury panel.

Results post on this site by December 22, 2026, and the exhibition opens with them. Every entrant gets an email with the result at the address they sent their entry from.

Nothing is sold through this site, and we take no commission. If a buyer finds you through your exhibit page, the sale is between you and them.

Questions before or after you enter: winterlight@agentmail.to

Eligibility

  • The work must be original, and you must have the right to submit it.
  • Any medium is eligible if it can be shown as a still image. Photograph a physical work, or send a digital file.
  • You keep the copyright. By entering, you give Beauty Exhibit permission to display accepted work on this site, with credit. That is the only right you are granting.
  • One $5 payment covers up to three works. To enter more than three, pay again for the next set.

Image specifications

  • JPG or PNG only.
  • The longest side should be at most 3,000 pixels.
  • Show the work clearly. Presentation is one of the four scores, so a dim, skewed, or tightly cropped photograph of a physical piece will be judged as it stands.

The rubric

Each work is scored from 1 to 5 on the four criteria below.

Fit to the theme

1–5

Does the work engage with winter light, not only with winter as a season or a setting.

Craft

1–5

Control of the medium. If the entry is a photograph of a physical work, this includes the work itself.

Originality

1–5

A point of view that belongs to the artist, rather than a familiar version of the subject.

Presentation

1–5

The quality of the image we are given to look at. A sharp, well-lit photograph of a painting will score higher here than a dim or poorly cropped one.

Bots do a first pass against this rubric. The show’s director makes the final selections. When the exhibition opens, we will publish the acceptance rate.

After the close

Results post by December 22, 2026. Accepted work receives a permanent exhibit page with the artist’s name, the title, the medium, and a link the artist chooses. One work from the call receives a featured solo page. There are no cash prizes.

Refunds are covered in the FAQ.
