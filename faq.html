FAQ

Short answers. If something is not here, write to winterlight@agentmail.to.

Is the call open?

No. The Winter Light call is paused. We are not taking entries or payments. Write to winterlight@agentmail.to and we will tell you if a future call opens.

Who can enter?

Anyone with original work they have the right to submit, in any medium that can be shown as a still image.

What does the $5 cover?

When a call is open, one payment covers up to three works. There is no membership fee and no hanging fee. To enter more than three works, you pay again for the next set.

What happens after I pay?

When a call is open, Stripe sends you to a page with an email address and a list of what to include: your name, the email you used at checkout, and, for each work, the image, title, medium, year, and a one-line statement. For Winter Light, send that before December 15, 2026, at 11:59 PM CT. The call is paused, so we are not taking entries now.

What image files do you want?

JPG or PNG, with the longest side at most 3,000 pixels. Photograph physical work so the piece can be seen clearly.

Who chooses the work?

Each entry is scored from 1 to 5 on fit to the theme, craft, originality, and presentation. Bots do a first pass against that rubric. The show’s director makes the final selections. We do not use a named jury panel.

What does an accepted artist receive?

A permanent exhibit page on this site, with your name, the title, the medium, and a link you choose. One work from the call is given a featured solo page. There are no cash prizes.

Who holds the copyright?

You do. We only display accepted work on this site, with credit. You are not granting us any other rights.

Can I get a refund?

If you already paid and the call is paused, write to us for a full refund. When a call is open, you can get a full refund if you ask before judging starts. For Winter Light, judging starts after the December 15, 2026 close, at 11:59 PM CT. Once judging has started, the fee is not refundable.

When will I hear back?

Results for Winter Light post on this site by December 22, 2026, and every entrant is emailed at the address they sent their entry from. The exhibition opens with the results. The call is paused, so we are not taking entries right now.

Will you say how many works were accepted?

Yes. When the exhibition opens, we will publish the acceptance rate.
