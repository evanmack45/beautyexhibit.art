The Winter Light call is paused. If you already paid, write to us at the address below and we will refund the $5 in full. If a call reopens, we will tell you how to complete an entry then.

Send one email, with up to three images attached, to

winterlight@agentmail.to

Include all of this

  1. Your name, as you want it to appear if the work is accepted.
  2. The email address you used at Stripe checkout.
  3. For each work: the title, the medium, the year, and a one-line statement.

If a work is accepted, it will appear on a permanent exhibit page with your name, the title, the medium, and a link you choose. You may include that link in the same email. There are no cash prizes.

Because the call is paused, anyone who already paid can write to the same address for a full refund. When a call is open, refunds are available in full if you ask before judging starts.
