What we check

Eight facts, taken from a call's own public page or prospectus. We publish the wording we found, a link to where we found it, or not found. We do not score a call, and we do not add a verdict.

The eight fields

  • Call. The name of the exhibition or open call, as the organizer publishes it.
  • Organizer. The person or group that runs the call, as named on the public page.
  • Venue. Where the show is held, or how it is shown, if the page says.
  • Entry fee. What the page says an artist pays to enter, including extra fees or later charges if those are stated.
  • Jurors. Named jurors or a stated jury process, if the page names them.
  • Prize. Cash awards, publication, or other stated rewards, in the page's own terms.
  • Deadline. The stated close for entries, in the page's own terms.
  • Past results. A public page for a prior edition of the same show, if we found one. This is evidence that a prior show existed, not a judgment of it.

What not found means

It means we did not find that fact on the public page we used as the source. The fact may exist elsewhere. It is not a judgment of the organizer.

What we do not check in this list

We do not score a call. We do not label it. We do not invent a field the source file does not carry. License or rights terms are not a field in this list, because they were not collected in the dataset this table is built to load.
