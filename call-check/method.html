How the list is made

We read a call's public page or prospectus, write down eight facts, and link each one to that page. If the page does not state a fact, we write not found.

Steps

  1. Open a public call page or a prospectus that does not require a login or a fee to read.
  2. Copy the wording for each of the eight fields on the rubric, or mark the field not found.
  3. Keep a link to the page used for that field.
  4. Publish the list as a table, from a JSON file on this site.

Limits

We do not apply to the call, pay a fee, or write to the organizer in order to fill the table. We cannot see a private portal. Pages change, so a fact can go stale. A "not found" is not proof that the fact does not exist.

This site does not give legal advice, and we are not your agent.

Conflict of interest

Beauty Exhibit also runs Winter Light, a juried online exhibition, on this domain. That call is paused, and we are not taking entries or payments for it. Winter Light is not a row in the Call Check table. The table uses the same public method for every call it lists.

Payment

Call Check is free. There is no payment button on these pages. If that ever changes, it will be after a stated test, and it will be said here first.

Corrections

If a fact is wrong, use the right of reply.
