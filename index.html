If a work is accepted

It gets a permanent exhibit page on this site, with your name, the title, the medium, and a link you choose. One work from the call will be given a featured solo page. There are no cash prizes.

How work is chosen

Each entry is scored from 1 to 5 on four published criteria: fit to the theme, craft, originality, and presentation. Bots do a first pass against that rubric. The show’s director makes the final selections.

When the exhibition opens, we will publish the acceptance rate. The full rubric is on the rules page.

Eligibility, in short

Submit work you made, and that you have the right to show. You keep the copyright. If a work is accepted, we display it here with credit, and that is the extent of the permission you are giving us.
